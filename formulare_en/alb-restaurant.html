Application form

Landings and take-offs within 100 m of a restaurant
Legal basis: Ordinance on Off-Airfield Landings (AulaV; SR 748.132.3) Art. 10, Art. 25(d), Art. 32(f) and Art. 34(e).
Fee based on time spent, pursuant to the FOCA Fees Ordinance (GebV-BAZL; SR 748.112.11) Art. 38(1-i).
Instructions: This form can be used either by a private pilot (1) or by a Swiss aviation company (2). The authorisation is valid only for helicopters registered in the Swiss aircraft register. A separate form must be used for each restaurant. Several forms may be submitted together.
Fill in the form, print, have it signed, send with enclosure to: heli@bazl.admin.ch
The form with original signatures must be kept by the applicant.
1) Non-commercial flights (to be completed by the pilot)
2) Commercial and/or training flights (to be completed by the aviation company)
Consent of the landowner and of the restaurant operator:
1) For a maximum of 2 landings per month by the private pilot named above or
2) For an unlimited number of landings by the company named above.
Note: responsibility for a safe landing always rests with the pilot.
