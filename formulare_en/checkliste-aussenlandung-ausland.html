General information
> Legal basis: Ordinance on Off-Airfield Landings (AulaV; SR 748.132.3).
> For commercial flights, Art. 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 19, 25 and 30 apply in particular; for non-commercial flights, Art. 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 19, 30 and 32.
> Consent of the landowner is a prerequisite.
> Landings in residential or nature-protection areas, above 1100 m AMSL, and at night are prohibited.
> Landings at a mountain landing site (GLP) are only possible with a Swiss MOU(H) pilot licence.
> Cross-border flights must be conducted via a customs airfield.
> If the landing takes place at an event, the organiser's consent is required.
> "High-risk SPO" flights additionally require a "cross-border high-risk authorisation" (see link).
> Link to SPO information: https://www.bazl.admin.ch/spo
> Link to aviation obstacle maps: https://map.geo.admin.ch/?topic=aviation
> Link to Swiss airspace restrictions (DABS): www.skybriefing.com
> Allow 10 working days for processing the application.
> The authorisation is granted for a maximum duration of 1 year.
> Fee CHF 500.- per authorisation (pursuant to Art. 38(1)(f)(1) GebV-BAZL; SR 748.112.11)
> The application must be submitted by the applicant by e-mail to heli@bazl.admin.ch.
