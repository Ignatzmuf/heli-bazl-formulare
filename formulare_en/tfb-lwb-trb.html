For Swiss operators, commercial helicopter flights under EASA Part-SPO
  1) Flying below minimum heights
  2) Avalanche blasting

Instructions: Fill in the form, print, sign, scan and send to: heli@bazl.admin.ch
1) Low-flying authorisation under SOP & high-risk authorisation
Pursuant to Art. 28 Rules of the Air Ordinance (VRV-L; SR 748.121.11) and SERA 5005(f) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 923/2012
Fee pursuant to Art. 38(e) FOCA Fees Ordinance (GebV-BAZL; SR 748.112.11)
2) Avalanche-blasting authorisation under SOP & high-risk authorisation
Pursuant to Art. 14 Civil Aviation Act (LFG; SR 748.0), Art. 81 Civil Aviation Ordinance (LFV; SR 748.01), Art. 9 Rules of the Air Ordinance (VRV-L; SR 748.121.11), Art. 16 Air Transport Ordinance (LTrV; SR 748.411)
Fee pursuant to Art. 38(c) FOCA Fees Ordinance (GebV-BAZL; SR 748.112.11)
3) Training authorisation with landings above 2000 m/AMSL
only for Swiss rescue organisations or the Swiss police
Pursuant to Art. 36 Ordinance on Off-Airfield Landings (AulaV; SR 748.132.3)
No fee pursuant to Art. 38(f)(3) FOCA Fees Ordinance (GebV-BAZL; SR 748.112.11)
